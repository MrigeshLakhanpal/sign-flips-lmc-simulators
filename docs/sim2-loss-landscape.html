If two solutions are secretly the same, why is there a hill between them?

Imagine two copies of a trained model that behave identically but sit at different spots in “weight space”. Walk straight from one to the other and the model gets worse in the middle. This page lets you rotate that landscape and find a way around.

Expert notes

PAPERLakhanpal (2026), v2, Sections 2, 4.2 and Appendix B (Lemma 1, Proposition 1).

What the toy borrows
At width r, the 2rr! permutation and sign-flip copies of an irreducible network are isolated points of the set of equivalent networks (Zhao et al.). With one spare unit, Proposition 1 joins them by a piecewise-linear path along which the function is exactly constant. Here the ring plays that role.
What it does not show
The path’s existence says nothing about the straight-line barrier, which is what the experiments measure. The true loss surface is high-dimensional; this 2-D height field is only a picture.
Barrier
B = supα[L(θα) − ((1−α)L(θA)+αL(θB))]. In the toy the straight line gives B = 1 at the midpoint.
