Technical statement

Networks follow f(x)=Σ sᵢσ(wᵢᵀx+bᵢ)+c with a sigmoid output and cross-entropy, Glorot-uniform initialisation, zero biases, full-batch Adam (lr 0.01, 3,000 steps). Alignment searches all 2hh! permutation and sign-flip combinations ((w,b,s,c) ↦ (−w,−b,−s,c+s)), chosen by midpoint loss on 400 points; barriers use 25 interpolation weights on all 1,000 points: B = max over α of [L(θα) − ((1−α)L(θA)+αL(θB))].

Evidence label: live mini-replication. This runs the preprint’s pipeline on simplified polygons with 1,000 points and one seed pair at a time. It is not the preprint’s data: the paper uses 100–120 seeds per task, irregular polygons, 2,000 points and a 2% inclusion rule, and reports minimal width 3 or 4 at 2% error and k at 1%. A network that misses 2% is not included in the paper’s results, and narrow networks often miss it. Scope: one hidden layer, sigmoid; ReLU has no sign flip. Source: Lakhanpal (2026), preprint v2, Sec. 3–5.
